Embedding the Pocket Cube State Explorer

This page stands in for a blog post. Its own styles (serif text, plain headings and buttons) do not reach into the explorer, and the explorer's styles do not leak out. The explorer below starts when it scrolls into view.

<script type="module" src="https://pocket-cube-state-explorer.vercel.app/embed/pocket-cube-explorer.js"></script>
<pocket-cube-explorer no-header></pocket-cube-explorer>
